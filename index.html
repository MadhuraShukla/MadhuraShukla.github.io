pune-job-tracker

pune-job-tracker is a personal finance job-search and resume-management tool. It helps its owner review job opportunities, organise application progress in Google Sheets, and store editable resumes in a private Google Drive folder.

The application requests Google Drive permission to create its resume folder, upload Word documents, and check the files it creates. It uses the limited drive.file permission. A separately authorised service account updates the owner's selected Google Sheet with job information, application tracking and resume links.

The application does not submit job applications, message recruiters or make resume files public. The owner reviews documents before applying.

Read the Privacy policy for information about data access and storage. For support, use the support email displayed on the application's Google consent screen.
